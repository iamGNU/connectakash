Hello, world: why I'm starting a blog

By Akash Anand · 9 November 2026 · 2 min read

  • Personal

Welcome to my blog. I'm Akash. You might know me from the rest of this site as an engineer, but this corner of it isn't about work. It's a place for anything and everything I come across in life.

Why a blog?

Every day brings small moments worth holding on to: a place that surprised me, a book or film that stayed with me, a conversation that changed how I see something, or just a passing thought. Most of them slip away. Writing them down helps me notice them, make sense of them and keep them.

What to expect

There's no fixed theme. Some posts might be about:

  • Places: trips, walks and the people I meet along the way.
  • Things I'm enjoying: books, films, music, food and whatever else catches my eye.
  • Lessons: things life taught me, sometimes the hard way.
  • Random thoughts: ideas and questions I can't stop thinking about.
  • The occasional bit of tech, because that's part of my life too.
Write about whatever comes along, and see where it goes.

Thanks for stopping by. If you'd like to say hello or share something you came across, you can reach me by email or on LinkedIn.
